Oman FOB 8-Stage Execution Workflow

Prescribed pipeline strictly governing bulk Urea N46 and Granular Sulfur allocations out of Oman export terminals.

01
ICPO Issue
Buyer issues Irrevocable Corporate Purchase Order with full corporate credentials.
02
SPA Contract
Seller issues Sales & Purchase Agreement (SPA). Both parties sign and seal.
03
POF Submission
Buyer provides Proof of Funds via certified bank confirmation letter or MT799.
04
PPOP & Visit
Seller provides Partial POP; buyer is cleared for terminal and tank verification.
05
DLC / TT Instrument
Buyer issues Documentary Letter of Credit (DLC MT700) or approved TT instrument.
06
SGS Inspection
Independent SGS / Saybolt conducts quality, weight, and grade tally.
07
Vessel Loading
Cargo loading onto buyer's chartered vessel under maritime laytime schedule.
08
B/L Payment
Final MT103 wire release executed against Clean On-Board B/L & SGS reports.
