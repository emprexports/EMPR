FOB Protocol: Sulfur & Urea (Oman)

Oman FOB 8-Stage Execution Workflow

Strict pipeline governing bulk Urea N46 and Granular Sulfur allocations out of Oman export terminals.

01
ICPO Issue
Buyer issues Irrevocable Corporate Purchase Order with full corporate and banking coordinates.
02
SPA Contract
Seller issues Sales & Purchase Agreement (SPA). Both parties sign and seal legally binding covenants.
03
POF Submission
Buyer provides Proof of Funds (POF) via bank confirmation letter or MT799/MT199 format.
04
PPOP & Terminal Visit
Seller provides Partial POP; buyer is cleared for terminal/cargo visual inspection and tank farm verification.
05
DLC / TT Instrument
Buyer issues Documentary Letter of Credit (DLC MT700) or approved TT financial instrument.
06
SGS Inspection
Independent SGS or Saybolt inspection board conducts comprehensive quality, weight, and grade tally.
07
Vessel Loading
Cargo loading onto buyer's chartered or nominated vessel in accordance with maritime laytime agreement.
08
B/L Payment
Final MT103 wire settlement executed against presentation of Clean On-Board Bill of Lading & SGS reports.
Global Delivery: CIF & CFR Framework

Standard ICC CIF Procedures (EN590, Georgia Sulfur, DRC Copper)

Executed under standard ICC Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP 600).

1. Verification & SPA

Buyer submits ICPO with corporate profile. Seller provides Full Corporate Offer (FCO) followed by mutually endorsed SPA.

2. Documentary Credit

Buyer's tier-one bank issues irrevocable DLC MT700 or standby SBLC MT760 to seller's fiduciary bank.

3. Maritime Transport & POP

Vessel chartered with 110% maritime cargo insurance. Complete Proof of Product (POP) transmitted via SWIFT.

4. Discharge & Settlement

SGS verification at safe discharge port. Settlement released via bank-to-bank MT103 upon certificate validation.
